# Customer Positioning

## Version Context

v1 tried to win as a broad repair-cost/pSEO product. That was the wrong customer frame because the traffic was too generic and the tool identity was too hidden.

v2 positions LifeVerdict as an inspection ask pre-send check. The product is built around one urgent transaction question: should this ask be sent, revised, cut down, or held until evidence/form/lender issues are clear?

## Primary Customer
Buyer agents and 2-10 seat buyer-agent teams handling financed inspection-response negotiations.

Why:
- They repeat this workflow across multiple live deals.
- They care about speed, wording, trust, and defensibility at the same time.
- They are closer to paying for workflow, export, and team features than broad consumer traffic.
- The best fit is first-time-buyer-heavy, FHA/VA/conventional financed deal flow where the inspection contingency is still active and the agent has to prove value clearly.

## Secondary Customer
Buyers under contract who want a stronger first draft before they send the packet through their agent workflow.

Why:
- They have urgent intent and high pain.
- They can activate the product directly.
- They should not pull the product into a broad DIY education site.

## Not The Customer Right Now
- Inspector report-writing businesses already living inside Spectora / HomeGauge / Palmtech style request-list workflows
- Lender or insurer workflow teams
- Broad homeowner / renovation / DIY traffic
- Contractor lead-generation flows
- Cash-heavy / luxury / waived-inspection buyer segments where inspection negotiation is not the main workflow bottleneck

## Deep Domain Pain
- The hard part is not reading the report. The hard part is deciding what not to ask for.
- The packet has to survive real deadline pressure, lender pressure, and seller pushback.
- A buyer agent is judged on whether the ask is credible, scoped, and sendable before the contingency window closes.
- Generic "repair request builders" already exist, so differentiation has to come from pre-send judgment: number basis, evidence packaging, seller pushback risk, financing-aware framing, form boundary, and send posture.

## Product Rule
If a feature does not make the buyer-agent ask easier to judge, defend, revise, or send, it is not core.

## Surface Rule
Search surfaces can vary by question:
- inspection response letter
- seller credit after inspection
- what to ask for after home inspection
- repair request after inspection
- inspection objection

But every surface must still converge on the same buyer-agent packet workflow.
